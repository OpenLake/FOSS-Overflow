Project Overview 💡  
The Centre-for-Career-Planning-and-Services Portal (CCPS Portal) is a comprehensive platform designed to streamline the placement and recruitment process. It connects students, alumni, and recruiters on a single platform, making job discovery, application tracking, and career development more efficient.  

The platform allows students to explore job opportunities, apply seamlessly, and manage their profiles, while administrators can manage job postings, alumni data, and recruitment workflows. Overall, it improves transparency, organization, and accessibility in the placement process.
