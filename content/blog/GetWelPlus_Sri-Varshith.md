# Project Idea 💡

GETWEL+ is a digital mental wellness platform designed to provide users with tools for managing their mental health effectively. The goal of the project is to create an accessible ecosystem where users can:

- Track their mood 📊  
- Book therapy sessions 🗓️  
- Attend online consultations 🎥  
- Read helpful articles 📚  
- Interact through chat systems 💬  

The platform also includes an **admin/doctor side interface** to manage patients, meetings, and communication efficiently.
